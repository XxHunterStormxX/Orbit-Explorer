## Short description

ORbit Explorer is an educational Mars-colony management game. Players manage the fictional Base Ares Planitia, balance food, water, oxygen and energy, develop infrastructure and respond to simulated events. The experience introduces mission-planning trade-offs through interactive decisions. A NASA DONKI panel can display recent CME analysis data when the service is available; this information is contextual and does not control the simulation.
